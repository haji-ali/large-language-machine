# Transport and setup

## Transport  
- ### **IMPORTANT** Before *and* after any kind of transport, **tighten all of the scaffold joints** with a 7mm allen key.
- Moving the wheels over bumpy ground or shaking about in a van can loosen the joints! Check them all before moving things around.
- Make sure that any parts that you're going to lift from are extra tight.
- After transport you might need to [open the rear panel](Repairs.md#rear-panel) and reposition the CRT monitor if it's moved out of position.
   
## Initial setup
1. Use the reusable cable ties to attach the basket to the **right-hand side** of the machine. Put all of the *Secret Rules* spheres in the basket.   
    - *It needs to go on the right – if you put it on the left-hand side then the UHF reader for the Secret Rules spheres will start detecting some of the spheres from inside the basket.*
    * ![image_1790334828958_0](files\image_1790334828958_0.png)
1. Place each of the *Question* cards on the small hooks along the front of the machine.
    * ![image_1790278335310_0](files\image_1790278335310_0.png)
1. Place the *Dataset* tubes into the metal brackets on the upper-right of the front panel.
    * ![image_1790334862059_0](files\image_1790334862059_0.png)
1. Follow the instructions for [powering on](Getting-started.md#powering-on) the machine.
